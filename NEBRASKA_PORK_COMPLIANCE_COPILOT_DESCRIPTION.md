# Nebraska Pork Compliance Copilot

The Nebraska Pork Compliance Copilot is a public, evidence-backed assistant for pork producers and farm managers. It searches an approved collection of Nebraska and applicable federal authorities before answering questions about livestock-waste permits, manure application, labor, animal health, worker safety, recordkeeping, and interstate market requirements.

Each answer separates the short answer, why the rule applies, controlling requirements, records to keep, missing farm facts, and official sources. The backend rejects unsupported citations and removes uncited claims. When the evidence does not support an answer, the tool says so instead of relying on model memory.

The Nebraska-first corpus includes current materials from the Nebraska Department of Water, Energy, and Environment, Nebraska Department of Agriculture, Nebraska Legislature, Nebraska Department of Labor, Nebraska Extension, USDA APHIS, U.S. Department of Labor, OSHA, FDA, and other government authorities that can affect a Nebraska pork operation.

Producers can add optional farm facts such as county, operation type, animal capacity, manure storage, worker relationship, and California sales. Those details improve applicability but are never treated as regulatory evidence. The public site requires no access code or account.

The product remains compliance decision support. It does not replace legal advice, veterinary diagnosis, emergency reporting, permits, inspections, or the producer's responsibility to confirm requirements with the responsible agency.
